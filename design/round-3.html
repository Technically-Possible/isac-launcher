Your call

Six decisions

Reply with the numbers. For anything you skip, I'll go with my pick.

  1. Which page opens when you press Home?

    Intel (your dashboard), Loadout, or the HUD.

    Intel, with Loadout and Inventory a swipe away.

  2. Slack: sign in, or keep your current widget?

    Signing in gives the styled status module. Your existing Status widget can sit in a widget slot instead.

    Try the sign-in. Fall back to your widget if work blocks it.

  3. Weather: phone location or a fixed town?

    Phone location follows you around. A fixed town needs no location permission.

    Approximate phone location.

  4. Photos: which folder, and how often?

    Both frames can share one folder or use one each. They can change every few minutes, every hour, or only when you tap.

    One folder, a new photo every 30 minutes, full color.

  5. App icons?

    White line icons like the game's items, original colors, or your hexagon icon pack. The launcher can read most icon packs.

    White on Loadout and HUD, with your icon pack as an option.

  6. Your callsign?

    "Kestrel" is a placeholder. You can change it in Settings anytime.

    Tell me yours.

Build: Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, Android 8.0 and newer. It installs as a normal app you choose as your home screen, and you can switch back anytime.
