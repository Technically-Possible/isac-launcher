SHD ISAC Launcher

Rebuilt from the game's own menus. One typeface in the style of the game's UI font, white text on dark glass over a blurred world, and orange only for selection and SHD marks. It also uses the game's item rarity colors and its verbs: equip, mark as junk, dismantle.

Build: Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, Android 8.0 and newer. It installs as a normal app you choose as your home screen, and you can switch back anytime.

About the look: a fan design inspired by Tom Clancy's The Division 2. All artwork and icons here are original. SHD and ISAC are Ubisoft's names, so this is fine for personal use but would need its own name for a public release.
